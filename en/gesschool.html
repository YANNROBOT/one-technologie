In short

Know who is there, who is late and which class did not take place

No more paper registers and end-of-month recounts. Each scan is checked, stamped with the official time and shown to management straight away.

Check-in by QR code

A poster or a screen at the entrance, a phone, one scan. Arrival is recorded in a second.

On site, with their own phone

The location is checked and each account is tied to one phone: nobody checks in for a colleague.

Lateness and missed classes, calculated

Check-in follows each teacher’s timetable. Lateness, early departures and classes not taught add up by themselves.

Works even without a connection

The software stays usable when the connection drops. Changes are sent as soon as it comes back.

How it works

From the QR code to the head’s screen, in four steps

  1. The school displays its QR code

    At the entrance, on a printed poster or on a screen.

  2. The employee scans it with the app

    They open the app on their phone and frame the QR code.

  3. GesSchool checks

    The right phone, on site, at the right moment of the timetable. The time kept is the official one, not the phone’s.

  4. Management sees it at once

    The check-in appears on the software’s home page, with the lateness if there is any.

The software

The head’s desk, on the computer

Installed on Windows like any other software, or opened in a browser. It updates itself: a window offers each new version.

Home: the day at a glance

  • Present, on time, late, not arrived, absent, excused.
  • Today’s classes: planned, taught, late, not taught.
  • Attendance by day, week or month.

Attendance, by day or over a period

  • For each employee: arrival, departure, lateness, start and end of each class.
  • Manual check-in when a phone is broken, recorded in the log.
  • Search, filters, Excel export and a clean printed sheet, with everyone’s photo.

Each teacher’s timetable

  • One grid per week: time slot, subject, class, room.
  • Two classes at the same time in the same room are refused.
  • Print a teacher’s timetable, a class’s, or everyone’s at once.

Check-in QR codes

  • Static QR: a poster to print and put at the entrance.
  • Dynamic QR: it changes every 30 seconds on a screen. A photo of the code is useless.
  • Display only: a tablet shows the QR code without giving access to the software.
  • Personal QR: each employee has their own code, which management scans on site.
