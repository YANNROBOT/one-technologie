Site de One Technologie
The need

Present the company, its services and its Education branch, and receive quote requests, in French and in English.

Our answer

A fast, light website, easy to read on a phone, in light or dark theme, with a quote form linked to the company mailbox.

What it does

What the website does

  • Two worlds: the company, in black and gold, and Education, in blue
  • Four-step quote request, sent by e-mail
  • Customer testimonials, published after approval
  • One page per project, with the tools used
  • In French and English
  • Light or dark theme
  • Scroll animations, turned off if the phone asks
  • Ready for Google: titles, descriptions and sitemap
