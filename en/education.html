Vacataire Pro

One timetable. Every school. No confusion.

A part-time teacher often works in several schools. Their timetables arrive as a photo, a PDF, a WhatsApp message or a word in the corridor, and sometimes clash. This app, planned for later under a provisional name, will gather all their classes in one calendar.

A single calendar

Every class, every group and every school in one view, by day, week or month.

Two ways to fill it

The teacher enters their hours, or the school sends them directly. A class received twice is never duplicated silently.

Clashes flagged

Two classes at the same time, or too little time to travel from one school to the other: the app warns before it becomes a problem.

On the phone

For the teacher

  • Add several schools and see them together.
  • Get a notification when a class is moved or cancelled.
  • Check the timetable without a connection.
  • Export the timetable as a PDF.
On the web

For the school

  • Invite its part-time teachers.
  • Send them their hours, by class and subject.
  • Change a class and explain why: the teacher is told.
  • See only its own data, never the teacher’s other schools.
Ideas for later

  • Pay notebook
  • Statement of hours taught
  • Lesson progress log
  • Part-time teaching offers
